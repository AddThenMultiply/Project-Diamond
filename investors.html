The ecosystem · buy-side and growth capital

An extensive network of institutional investor relationships

Preparation only pays when it reaches the right people. Behind the platform sits an extensive, curated network of institutional relationships (private equity, growth capital and strategic acquirers) maintained so that ready businesses reach the right conversations quickly.

How the relationships break down

Private equity & buyout

Majority and buyout investors focused on established, profitable founder-led businesses.

Growth capital

Minority growth investors backing proven businesses scaling to their next stage.

Strategic buyers

Trade acquirers seeking capability, market position and consolidation opportunities.

How routing works, and who does it

The diagnostic and roadmap prepare you; they do not market you. When a business is transaction-ready, introductions to investors and buyers are made exclusively through ATM's specialist, authorised transaction partner as part of a Readiness Project engagement. The platform itself does not arrange investments, promote investment opportunities or share your information with any investor. Warm, well-matched introductions are the point: research shows deals reaching an investor through a warm introduction are thirteen times more likely to reach the investment committee.
