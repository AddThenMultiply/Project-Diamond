How routing works — and who does it

The diagnostic and roadmap prepare you; they do not market you. When a business is transaction-ready, introductions to investors and buyers are made exclusively through ATM's specialist, authorised transaction partner as part of a Readiness Project engagement. The platform itself does not arrange investments, promote investment opportunities or share your information with any investor. Warm, well-matched introductions are the point: research shows deals reaching an investor through a warm introduction are thirteen times more likely to reach the investment committee.
