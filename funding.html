Funding readiness, done properly

Backing female founders at the same level as their male counterparts is an $8.7 trillion global opportunity — yet the playing field remains uneven. Deals reaching a venture firm through a warm introduction are thirteen times more likely to reach the investment committee. Preparation and routing are everything. If your plan is strong, steady growth rather than unicorn-or-bust, angels and family offices are often a better fit than venture capital. Add debt funding and grants to the picture and the map widens further — that conversation is exactly what the Readiness Call is for.

3 · The promotion test — check your pitch language

Research by Dr Dana Kanze (London Business School) shows investors ask female founders prevention questions (safety, protection, stability) and male founders promotion questions (growth, vision, acquisition) — and founders who answer every question in promotion language raise up to fourteen times more. Paste a paragraph of your pitch or grant narrative; this checks it in your browser.
