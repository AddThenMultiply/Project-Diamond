Insourcing · Finance Advisory · the Consolidate phase of FACE · step 03

Finance Advisory, embedded in your business

Most readiness gaps are finance gaps: no clear picture of the numbers, no finance leader who has been through a transaction, no reporting a buyer or investor would trust. We start with a Financial Deep Dive to establish the true position. Then we insource what the roadmap calls for — a CFO or a whole finance function, embedded in your business rather than bolted on beside it — and behind that, the wider fractional and specialist bench below. No more than you need, and all of it yours to keep.

How it runs

1 · Financial Deep Dive

The true picture first: quality of earnings, cash, margin, the model, the reporting. What a buyer's advisers would find, found by yours.

2 · Insourced CFO or finance function

A transaction-experienced finance leader, or the function around one, embedded on a defined 90-day sprint with deliverables the roadmap set.

3 · The wider bench

The fractional executives, systems and advisory strength in the table below, sourced only where the Deep Dive and the roadmap show a gap.

Why insource rather than outsource?

Buyers pay for businesses that own their capability. Skills embedded in your team and systems installed in your business raise your value; dependencies on external providers do not. Everything we source is designed to be yours when the engagement ends.
