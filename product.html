Product · Key Person of Influence · feeds Consolidate, leading to Exit

A business that runs on assets, not on you

The hardest thing about a founder-led business is the founder. Your judgement, your relationships and your hours are what made it work — and they are exactly what a buyer cannot buy and an investor cannot bank on. The Product stream turns what only you can do into things the business owns, sells and repeats.

Take the free ten-minute self-assessment ↓ — it tells you where you actually need help, and who should take the call.

Who this is for

The founder who is the product

Clients buy you. Every proposal is bespoke, every delivery needs you in it, and the diary is the ceiling on growth.

The business with know-how in people's heads

The method works, but it is not written down, and the person who knows it best could resign on Friday.

The founder planning an exit in two to three years

You want a price that reflects the business, not a discount that reflects your indispensability.

The problem

Revenue tied to the founder's time. Intellectual property nobody has documented. Little recurring revenue, so every year starts at zero. These are the things a buyer discounts and an investor prices down, and they usually surface at the worst moment: in diligence, after the headline number has been agreed. A buyer's question is simple — what happens to this business the day you leave? — and if the honest answer is "it gets smaller", the price already knows.

What we do

A ninety-day sprint that takes the four value levers a founder-dependent business is missing and builds them, in the order that moves your valuation soonest.

What you walk away with

  • A business that runs on assets rather than your effort. Products, a recurring model, documented IP and systems that belong to the company, not the person.
  • The value levers, on the roadmap. Each dependency you remove shows up as a value lever in your personalised roadmap, quantified against the diagnostic and re-scored as it moves.
  • A better answer to the only question a buyer really asks. What happens when you leave? The answer becomes: it keeps going.

Where it fits

Product is one of the influence assets from the Key Person of Influence method, and it sits squarely in the Consolidate phase of FACE: the work of making the business coherent, documented and ownable before it goes to market. It leads directly to Exit, because a business that runs without its founder is what a buyer is actually buying. In the value stream, the diagnostic tells you how founder-dependent you are today, the roadmap sequences the levers, and this sprint builds them alongside the insourced finance function that will report on them.
