Decisions

Seven things to ask David, as CEO of ATM

Ranked by what blocks launch. Each comes with a recommendation so the answer can be a yes, a no, or a change.

1Is this the regulatory wording ATM will stand behind?

Every page now says: Add Then Multiply is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. When a client is raising money or buying a business, David leads that work as an officer of the client company, for and on behalf of that company. Preparing a business for sale is not, in itself, a regulated activity. The platform never arranges investments and never publishes financial promotions.

Recommendation: confirm as written, including the footer legal line on every page. One word from David and it is final; until then nothing can go live.

2Who is the transaction partner, and can we name the relationship?

Investor introductions happen only offline, through ATM's partner. The site says "specialist transaction partner" throughout because we do not hold the partner's FCA firm reference or the agreement. If David can supply both, the wording can become "authorised transaction partner" in one edit.

Recommendation: keep "specialist" for launch; David sends the reference and agreement when he is ready and we change it then.

3Are the six door names and the turnover tiers final?

Doors: Funding Scorecard · Grow by Acquiring · Commercial Acceleration · Insourcing: Finance Advisory · Pitching · Exit Ready. Tiers: Project One £1m to under £20m · Project Two £20m to under £200m · Scaling £200m and above. Names lock into web addresses and the booking codes, so this is the moment to change them.

Recommendation: sign off both as they stand. Turnover guides the initial routing; the Readiness Call confirms need and complexity, and the copy says so.

4Which numbers will David put his name to?

Published: the 3,000 founder CEO network (owner-verified; we need the date and source to cite). Not published: any investor count, until David gives a verified figure. Cited from the books: the thirteen-times warm-introduction claim (primary source wanted) and the $8.7tn opportunity, now labelled an ATM extrapolation.

Recommendation: David gives the date behind 3,000 and the source behind thirteen times; everything else stays as it is.

5What does the report email say it is from, and who sees it?

Founders who opt in get their report by email. We need the sender address (for example reports@addthenmultiply.com), whether David wants a copy of every report, and confirmation of the 24-month retention period in the privacy notice.

Recommendation: a reports@ address on the ATM domain, a copy to one adviser inbox rather than David's personal one, 24 months.

6How and when does this go live?

Two routes: the new front door replaces addthenmultiply.com, or it runs alongside on a subdomain for a period with the existing site untouched. Either needs a date, whoever controls the domain, and a first-week plan for the Readiness Calls that will follow.

Recommendation: a subdomain first, for two to four weeks of real founders, then replace. Set the date today.

7Where does David want the instruments to go deeper?

Four items in Marcus's review need David's own definition before they can be built: the Funding Scorecard's treatment of questions that do not apply to a business; buyer and seller versions of the Ethical Acquisitions Scorecard; sector variations on Transaction Readiness; and a sample Roadmap deliverable. None blocks launch.

Recommendation: launch without them and schedule a one-hour working session with David on each after the first founders have been through.
