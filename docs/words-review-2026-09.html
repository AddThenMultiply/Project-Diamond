The words review: consulting speak, for David to tick or cross

21 September 2026. You said a couple of phrases read as consulting speak. This is every candidate we could find across the seventeen public pages, with where it appears, what it is doing there, and a plainer version. For each one choose keep, change, or give us your own words. At the end, one button copies your answers so you can paste them into an email. Nothing on this page is saved anywhere; if you leave it, your ticks go with it, so finish in one sitting or print the page and mark it by hand.

How this was done

The sweep

We read the visible text of every public page, including the questions, the written reports and the email copy, against a list of the words consultancies lean on: leverage, drive, unlock, robust, holistic, stakeholder, deliverable, workstream, optimise, synergy, ecosystem, value stream, end-to-end, alignment, enable, empower, framework, actionable, strategic, capability, streamline, playbook, cadence, and about thirty more. Most were absent. What follows is what was there, worst first, then the words that are ATM's own and only need confirming.

Two things are deliberately not in the list. Investor and buyer vocabulary that a founder must learn anyway (data room, due diligence, heads of terms, traction, comparable rounds) and the regulatory wording, which is Eversheds-cleared and yours to change only on advice.
