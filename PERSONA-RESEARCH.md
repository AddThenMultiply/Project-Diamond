# Persona Research — Founder & Capital Side (evidence base for the input engine)

Compiled August 2026 from 18 searches across founder sentiment, exit readiness, capital-side criteria and deal-failure evidence. Every claim cited. This document is the evidence behind the intake and mandate fields (migrations 003–004).

## Headline numbers that shape the product

- Only **20–30% of small businesses that go to market actually sell** ([EPI/Project Equity](https://project-equity.org/news/employee-ownership-insider/business-owner-statistics-exit-planning/)).
- **~47% of dead deals die because the buyer looked closely and didn't like what they found** — non-QoE diligence findings 25.3% + QoE/EBITDA discrepancies 21.3% (Axial Dead Deal Report via [CT Acquisitions](https://ctacquisitions.com/guides/why-ma-deals-fall-apart-2026/)).
- **30–50% of LOI-stage deals are renegotiated downward**, usually on quality-of-earnings findings ([Boxwood Partners](https://www.boxwoodpartners.com/press-releases/how-quality-of-earnings-impacts-your-sale-priceand-how-to-get-ahead-of-it)).
- Sell-side QoE preparation is worth **≈ +0.4x EBITDA** on the multiple ([CLA](https://www.claconnect.com/en/resources/blogs/private-equity/how-a-sellside-quality-of-earnings-can-help-close-a-business-sale)).
- PE screens hard at **~15% single-customer concentration**; >30% is typically a walk-away or a 20–35% valuation cut ([Beancount](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/05/11/customer-concentration-risk-10-percent-revenue-threshold-business-valuation-loan-capacity-negotiating-leverage-guide)).
- First-time acquirers succeed **~23% vs 54%** for serial acquirers ([40,000-deal study](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/analyzed-40-000-m-deals-202657786.html)).
- **~75% of owners report profound regret within a year of selling** — for emotional, not financial reasons ([Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/building-a-business/75-of-founders-regret-selling-their-business-within-a-year-heres-how-to-walk-away-with-more-than-just-money)); 78% lack a formal transition team ([Exit Planning Institute](https://exit-planning-institute.org/2023-national-state-of-owner-readiness)).
- Awareness of finance options is the **#1 barrier** to external finance for UK SMEs (69%); 51% are "permanent non-borrowers" ([British Business Bank](https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/about/research-and-publications/small-business-finance-markets-report-2025/sme-intermediary-research-uk-findings)).

## Founder persona — growth by acquisition

**Needs:** awareness of funding options; a lender-credible combined-entity story (projected DSCR ≥1.25x, ~2 years' profitability, debt/equity <3:1 — [Stable Payments](https://www.stablepayments.co.uk/blog/what-funding-options-are-out-there-to-help-a-uk-sme-acquire-another-business)); structuring help — deferred consideration is "one of the most common features in UK SME deals" ([Price Bailey](https://www.pricebailey.co.uk/guides/how-to-finance-acquisitions/)).
**Wants:** to buy now — ~50% of UK SME entrepreneurs think it's a good time to buy ([Marktlink Monitor](https://www.marktlink.com/uk/insights/new-survey-unveils-strong-m-a-confidence-among-european-sme-entrepreneurs)); buy-and-build accepted by investors even at 3–7% organic growth where bolt-on potential is clear ([CT Acquisitions](https://ctacquisitions.com/lower-middle-market-private-equity-2026/)).
**Fears:** additional debt (debt aversion is a top stated barrier); lender rejection; personal guarantees; the failure odds above.
**Struggles to produce:** normalised pro-forma combined EBITDA; defined acquisition criteria and a named integration owner (absence predicts abandonment — [CABB](https://cabb.org/node/1670)); financeability evidence.

## Founder persona — exit-seeking

**Needs:** exit education (68% now get it, up from 35% in 2013) and a transition team (78% lack one); a current valuation baseline (40% have none); pre-emptive sell-side QoE ([EPI](https://exit-planning-institute.org/2023-national-state-of-owner-readiness), [CLA](https://www.claconnect.com/en/resources/blogs/private-equity/how-a-sellside-quality-of-earnings-can-help-close-a-business-sale)).
**Wants:** buyer-type fit, not just price — family offices pay ~0.5–1.0x lower headline multiples but offer rollover, seller financing and post-close autonomy ([CT Acquisitions](https://ctacquisitions.com/family-office-vs-private-equity/)); legacy and team protection; minority models (BGF: no control stake, no exit deadline — [BGF](https://www.bgf.co.uk/minority-investment/)).
**Fears:** not selling at all; regret and identity loss (the strongest predictor of process abandonment is emotional unreadiness — [EPI](https://blog.exit-planning-institute.org/emotional-considerations-transitions), [Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/leadership-diversity-and-wellness/202603/the-hidden-grief-of-selling-your-business)); being locked in by earnouts and 2–3-year transitions ([SE Advisory](https://www.se-adv.com/industry-insights/founder-dependency-hidden-valuation-killer)).
**Why they abandon:** unrealistic valuation expectations; second thoughts at critical stages; inflexibility on structure; slow/inaccurate information supply; diligence surprises ([CABB](https://cabb.org/node/1670)).

## The design principle the evidence supports

The platform's core value is converting the ~47% of failures caused by diligence surprises into **pre-disclosed, structured data**: capture the founder's uncomfortable fields early (customer concentration, founder dependency, EBITDA adjustments, valuation basis, skeletons, emotional readiness) and hold them against the capital side's quantified tolerance thresholds — because prepared sellers earn measurably higher multiples and completion rates. Audited/reviewed financials measurably reduce the information-asymmetry discount ([EFMA](https://efmaefm.org/0efmameetings/efma%20annual%20meetings/2016-Switzerland/Papers/EFMA2016_0204_fullpaper.pdf), [Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jcaf.22782)).

**Field mapping:** every top-ranked field from this research is implemented in `founder_intake` and `investor_mandates` (migrations 003 + 004) and surfaced in `intake.html` / `investor-mandates.html`.

*Caveats: no single BDO/Grant Thornton exit-intentions survey found; Morgan Stanley founder-sentiment data is US/Canada VC-stage (adjacent evidence); 70–90% "failure" stats measure post-completion value destruction, distinct from process collapse.*
